# Kava 101

Kava is a root from the Pacific Islands. People have been drinking it socially
for centuries — the way other cultures use coffee, tea, or wine. The active
compounds in kava are called *kavalactones*. Noble kava (the kind we use) is a
specific cultivar group selected for daily-use safety and a clean experience.

## What it feels like

Most people describe a sense of physical calm — shoulders drop, jaw unclenches,
talkative. Mind stays clear. Sol pouches deliver that same feel in a take-home
format, without the bowl.

## Is kava the same as kratom? Or CBD?

No. Kava, kratom, and CBD are three completely different plants with different
compounds and effects. Sol is kava only — noble root extract, nothing else.

## Is it safe?

Noble kava has a long traditional-use history. As with anything you ingest:
moderation matters, and if you're pregnant, nursing, on medication, or have
liver concerns, talk to your doctor first. Sol is for adults 18+.
